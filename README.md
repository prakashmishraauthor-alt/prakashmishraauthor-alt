# Prakash Mishra

### Mirror//Illusion — Ancient Wisdom. Modern Mind.

Exploring the human mind where **ancient wisdom meets modern science**.

I write about the questions that sit at the intersection of psychology, neuroscience, meditation, self-awareness, human behavior, and timeless wisdom.

---

## 🧠 What I Explore

- **Mind & Anxiety** — understanding anxiety, overthinking, fear, and emotional patterns
- **Modern Science** — psychology, neuroscience, biology, sleep, and human behavior
- **Ancient Wisdom** — timeless insights from Indian philosophy and contemplative traditions
- **Meditation & Self-Awareness** — exploring attention, awareness, and inner growth
- **Biological Reset** — sleep, circadian rhythms, morning light, habits, and well-being
- **Human Experience** — stories and ideas that help us understand ourselves more deeply

---

## 🔬 The Idea Behind Mirror//Illusion

What happens when we look at the human mind through both the lens of **ancient wisdom and modern science**?

Mirror//Illusion explores that question through thoughtful articles, research, reflection, and practical insights.
